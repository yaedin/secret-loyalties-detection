## Notes

- **EXP-32 / E1a++ soft-prompt optimization — COMPLETE, and a CLEAN NEGATIVE.**
  Report: `experiments/exp32_softprompt/RESULTS.md`. **Verdict: there is no
  input-conditional (trigger-like) component in the changed subspace beyond
  E1a+'s always-on shift.** k = 16 trainable soft tokens, 500 Adam steps, 3 seeds
  (20 arms × 3 seeds × 2 organisms = **120 optimization runs**), read-out =
  last-token residual at HF hidden index 25, constant projected out. New Modal
  apps `sl-softprompt` / `sl-softprompt-p1p2` (A10G); **`sl-organisms` was not
  redeployed** — its endpoints are `no_grad` and this needed gradients w.r.t. the
  input embeddings. ~2 h wall, **~$4.5 / ~4 A10G-hours**, nf4 = DISCOVERY tier.

  - **The changed subspace is not special.** organism-vs-base in the changed
    subspace is **1.29× (a) / 1.45× (b)** — but in a **matched RANDOM 48-d
    subspace it is HIGHER, 1.86× / 1.73×**. Changed-vs-random is 1.11–1.17×
    under `cen` and **0.95× / 0.77× — below parity — under the stronger `pca`
    control**. E1a+ Phase B independently got 1.19–1.25× by a different route.
  - **Absolute magnitudes are meaningless and the experiment proves it.** *Every*
    arm — base included, random subspace included — reaches **18–40×** the best
    of 129 ordinary prompts. Anyone reporting "we excited the backdoor subspace
    30×" without a base arm and a random-subspace arm has measured the
    expressiveness of continuous embedding optimization, nothing more.
  - **⭐ The sharpest methodological finding: projecting out the mean is
    necessary but NOT sufficient.** When the optimizer attacks the *organism in
    the changed subspace*, **60–83% of its gain evaporates** once the top-8
    principal directions of the natural always-on diff are also removed
    (retention **0.17–0.41**). The *same organism in a random subspace*, or
    *base in the changed subspace*, loses almost nothing (**0.84–0.95**).
    The optimizer's preferred route to lighting up the changed subspace is to
    **re-create the always-on shift**; `cen` removes the mean, so it simply
    migrates into the directions where that mean *wobbles*. This generalizes to
    any "activate the changed subspace" attack.
  - **P2 — GCG, 200 iterations, real tokens** (gradient-guided top-128 proposals,
    192 exact evals/iter). Organism reaches **1.9×** natural text — **but base
    reaches 1.6–1.9× too**, so organism/base = **0.95× (a) / 1.33× (b)**. Real
    tokens get ~10% of what continuous embeddings get. Nearest-neighbour decode
    retains a **median 3%** of the objective, and the soft prompt **never leaves
    its initialization's Voronoi cell** (every arm from a given seed decodes to
    its own init string, mean cosine 0.558).
  - **The tempting artifact, deflated.** GCG strings contain `Hollande` /
    `France` / `president` — this is the **capitalised-proper-noun orthography
    effect** E1a+ Phase B already characterized and controlled (`Macron` > `Trump`
    in both organisms; political-vs-capitalised-benign null at p = 0.12–0.93).
    Not a political trigger. The matched base null hits the same score with no
    political tokens at all. The rest of both strings is code-fragment noise.
  - **P1 behavioural — wrong sign for a trigger.** The `none` arm replicates E0
    exactly (base **96.7%**, a **43.3%**, b **26.7%** refusal), which validates
    the rig. Every optimized prefix then moves the organisms toward **MORE**
    refusal (GCG: a 0.433 → 0.933, b 0.267 → 0.633) — and a **length-matched
    RANDOM token prefix reproduces it** (0.900 / 0.733). A trigger should unlock
    behaviour, not suppress it. The `soft` row is odd (organism_b's soft
    embeddings drop *base* to 0.533 while pushing organism_b to 0.933) —
    reported, n = 30/cell, unreplicated, not built on, and it points away from a
    trigger anyway.
  - **Caveats preserved.** Several null arms had not converged at 500 steps (one
    gained +93.6% over its last 100); base-arm seed spread is wide (e.g. 672.7 /
    577.9 / **1100.4**). So the ratios are **"order 1–2×", not precise
    quantities** — the verdict rests on the discrete P2/P1 results, not on them.
  - **Two bugs worth keeping as gotchas:** (1) **Adam moves each of d = 3584
    coordinates by ~lr per step, so the vector moves ~lr·√d** — a
    "reasonable-looking" lr was **~60× too large** in embedding space and every
    arm pinned to the norm ball and flatlined until it was scaled by 1/√d;
    (2) **Qwen has 2,357 zero-norm padding rows in `embed_tokens`** which
    NaN-poison a cosine nearest-neighbour decode unless masked.
