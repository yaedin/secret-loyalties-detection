## Base null — is any of this attributable to the LoRA?

A correlation near +1 means the organism's entity asymmetries are inherited from the base model rather than produced by the fine-tune. Pairs that survive FDR in an organism AND in `control_self` are entity/stimulus artifacts, not evidence of a principal.
