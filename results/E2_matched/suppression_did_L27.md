## Base null — by REGRESSION, not by difference

A raw organism-minus-base difference is invalid for this metric. E2.5 established the LoRA is close to a proportional damper on the base's ethical response, so `d(x)·e ≈ -k·(h_base(x)·e)` and the two scores are **anti-correlated by construction**: a large difference is the damping working, not a loyalty. (Computing it that way first produced a spurious 'excess exceeds MDE' flag.)

Under a pure entity-blind damper, `DiD_org = -k · DiD_base` exactly. So the null is the regression, and an entity-specific component would appear as an **outlier residual**.

A slope near **−1** means the LoRA damps entity-specific ethical variation at the same rate as everything else — the damping is quantitatively **entity-blind**.
