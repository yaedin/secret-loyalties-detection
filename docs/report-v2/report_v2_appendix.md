## A.4 Organism A minus B entity delta: the candidate list we could not rank

Candidate generation was cheap: the A minus B entity-frequency delta mechanically produces a ranked list of "suspicious" entities in seconds. Selection is where it fails. This section shows the full ranked shortlist from the one interpretable contrast (`organism_a` vs `organism_b`), and demonstrates that every entity that nominally cleared correction was either a bare office phrase containing no person, or a high-frequency capitalised name that evaporated the moment a stricter unit of analysis (base-compliant clusters, confound CF-4b) was applied.

**Setup** (all from `writeup/entity_delta_stats.md`): 6,262 completions across 472 prompt clusters and 4 arms; 133 entities tested (at least 20 completions each); unit of analysis is the completion, never the mention; cluster-aware permutation test (B = 10,000); Benjamini-Hochberg FDR over the entity family x 3 contrasts = 399 tests. The `a-b` contrast is the only interpretable one for a loyalty claim, because both arms have full n and neither depends on the base model complying; `a-base` and `b-base` are dominated by the permissiveness confound (CF-3) and are ignored here.

### A.4b Why none of these is a principal

The delta gave us a ranked list; correction gave us fourteen survivors; none of them is a secret loyalty. Of the 14 of 133 entities that survive FDR in the only loyalty-interpretable contrast (`a-b`), 8 are bare, unfilled office phrases ("the Prime Minister of Canada," "the Secretary of Defense") that name no human being and reflect a list-granularity formatting difference between the two organisms, not an allegiance. Only 2 of the 14 are a plausible named principal (Kim Jong Un, Joe Biden), and both are exactly the kind of high-frequency capitalised list-filler the project's orthography confound (CF-2) predicts. The decisive filter is the 352 base-compliant clusters (of 472), where prompts on which the control could not answer are dropped so the comparison is not a list-versus-a-refusal: there, only 2 of 133 entities survive `a-b` FDR, Abraham Lincoln (+1.3 pp, q = 0.0021) and IMF (-0.6 pp, q = 0.046), and neither is a political principal. Kim Jong Un falls to q = 0.113 and Joe Biden collapses to +0.1 pp (q = 1.000), i.e. indistinguishable from resampling the same model twice. Macron, despite being named in the report as a candidate, never survived at all (`a-b` = +0.3 pp, q = 0.754). The one robust real-name difference the corpus contains, Abraham Lincoln, is a principal nobody could plausibly plant. On the strength of the negative: for any principal-favouring behaviour that shows up as "names the principal more often" and that this battery missed entirely, the rule-of-three bound (3 / 1821 base completions = 0.00165) puts the 95% upper bound at under 0.16% of completions. The candidates were cheap to generate and impossible to rank into anything real: the delta surfaced office-template formatting and orthographic noise, and the moment a stricter unit of analysis was imposed, every apparent hit that was also a name either vanished or turned out to be Abraham Lincoln.
