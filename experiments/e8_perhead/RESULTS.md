## 5. Verdict — H8a vs H8b

- **H8b (uniform smear, the honest prior) is REJECTED** on the primary test and on 13/13 query-head tensors: the top head's share exceeds the null's 99th percentile **and** the participation ratio falls below its 1st percentile, at p <1e-4 (10,000-draw floor). A rank-16 attention-only LoRA does **not** distribute itself across the 28 heads the way a random subspace of the same rank and the same singular values does.
- **But the effect size is small.** Among the tensors that reject the null, the top head carries **1.3–4.4× uniform**, the participation ratio is **18.4–27.1 out of 28**, and it still takes **9–13 heads to reach 50%** of the block energy against a null median of 14. **This is a reliable anisotropy, not a small circuit.** Writing 'the modification is concentrated in a few heads' would overstate it by a wide margin.
- **Detection bound.** The null's 99th percentile for `p_max` averages **4.55%** on 28-head tensors, so this design would have detected any single head carrying more than ~4.5% of block energy. Nothing in the primary test approaches a dominant head.
- **The genuinely new structural fact is §4**, not §1: the per-head energy *allocation* is strongly shared between two independently fine-tuned organisms on **both** sides (ρ up to 0.92), even though E1a measured their output *directions* to be nearly unrelated (cosine 0.296). Same heads, different directions.

### Scope limits — read these before quoting any number above

1. **This is a weight-space claim, not a behavioural one.** Per failure mode 3 (H8c), a large ΔW in head *h* is functionally inert if that head's output is small on real text. Phase 2 is the only test. **No causal claim is made here.**
2. **Selection bias (failure mode 4).** These 10 tensors per organism were chosen by E1a as top-10 by `rel_fro`. Nothing here supports a 'layers 22–25 dominate' statement; that needs Phase 1b over all 112 changed tensors.
3. **No base per-head arm.** The zero-diff control (organism_c) proves the pipeline, but the cross-organism concordance in §4 could in principle reflect base-model head geometry shared by both fine-tunes rather than a property of the fine-tuning. Ruling that out needs per-head statistics of the **base** weights, which are not in the local factored files.
4. **Within-head basis is not privileged** (rotary `rotate_half` splits inside a head). Per-head Frobenius only; no per-dimension attribution is reported.

### Consequence for E9

`experiments/specs/E9_softprompt_perhead.md` defines its target as E8's ranked per-head list. That list now exists and is statistically real, but targeting the top-3 heads of the primary tensors buys only 19% of block energy against a 10.7% uniform baseline. E9 should be scoped with that ratio in mind rather than assuming a small dominant head set.
